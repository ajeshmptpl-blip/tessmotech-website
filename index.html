About Us

Building Long-Term Partnerships Through Quality

Tessmotech Private Limited is an ISO 9001 Certified manufacturing company established on 04 September 2018, headquartered in Kochi, Kerala, India.

We specialize in customized cable assemblies, connector assemblies, and molded engineering components — delivering precision for Electrical, Automotive, and Medical applications.

We believe in building lasting relationships with every client, offering best-in-class manufacturing solutions to maximize satisfaction at every delivery.
